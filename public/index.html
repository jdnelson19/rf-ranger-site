About

RF Ranger is built for live production teams who need to know, before doors open, that every mic is charged, switched on and clean. It finds your receivers on the network by itself and opens in a browser, so the RF desk, the A1, stage management and a TV by the mic table can all watch the same live picture.

Every channel at a glance

Green is ready, orange is off or on the charger, red is a low battery or a fault. Show presets put just the mics for this room or this segment on screen, with a nickname for each: RF-4 is "Sam" tonight.

What just happened?

A five-second audio waveform with the RF signal underneath. If audio drops out with the RF, it's a wireless problem. If audio spikes while RF holds steady, someone bumped the mic.

A pickup screen for performers

Put it on a TV by the mic table. Performers find their name, see which mic or pack is theirs, and whether it's ready.

Supported hardware
  • Shure Axient Digital receivers
  • Shure ULX-D receivers
  • Shure SLX-D receivers
  • Shure SBC220 and SBC240 networked chargers
  • Shure PSM 1000 in-ear transmitters (early support)
  • Sennheiser is planned. Using something else? Tell us below.
