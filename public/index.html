About

RF Ranger is for church and live-production teams who want to know, before the service starts, that every mic is charged, switched on and healthy. It finds your Shure receivers on the network by itself and opens in your browser, so any laptop, iPad or TV on the same network can show it.

Every channel at a glance

Green is ready, orange is off or on the charger, red is a low battery or a fault. Show presets put just the mics you need for worship, the message or announcements on screen, and give each one a nickname for the day: RF-4 is "Josh" this week.

What just happened?

A five-second audio waveform with the RF signal underneath. If audio drops out with the RF, it's a wireless problem. If audio spikes while RF holds steady, someone bumped the mic.

A pickup screen for the team

Put it on a TV by the RF table. Performers find their name, see which mic or pack is theirs, and whether it's ready. No more "which one's mine?" five minutes before downbeat.

Hardware
Shure ULX-D, Axient Digital and SLX-D receivers; SBC220 and SBC240 networked chargers. PSM 1000 in-ears are early. Sennheiser is planned.
Runs on
macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). No admin rights needed to install.
Cost
Free and open source (MIT). Source on GitHub.
Status
Early release: tested on Shure Axient Digital and improving quickly. Your feedback shapes what comes next.
